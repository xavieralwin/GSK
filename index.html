Key Safety & Prescribing Information³

Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to beta-lactams (e.g. penicillins, cephalosporins). Augmentin-associated jaundice/hepatic dysfunction.

Special Warnings & Precautions

Careful enquiry concerning previous hypersensitivity reactions. Anaphylactic reactions require immediate emergency treatment with adrenaline.

Qualitative & Quantitative Composition

AUGMENTIN 625 Duo: Amoxycillin 500mg + Clavulanic Acid 125mg. AUGMENTIN 1g Duo: Amoxycillin 875mg + Clavulanic Acid 125mg.
